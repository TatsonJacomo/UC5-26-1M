
FRASE NO PASSADO

I WAS a teacher -> Eu ERA/FUI um professor.
You WERE my friend -> Você ERA/FOI meu amigo. 
He WAS in a happyday ->Ela ESTAVA em um dia feliz.
She WAS very cool -> Ela ERA muito legal.
It WAS a summy day -> FOI/ERA um dia ensolarado.
We WERE all friends here -> Nós ÉRAMOS/FOMOS todos amigos aqui.
They WERE a tall and pround people. -> Eles ERAM/FORAM um povo alto e orgulhoso.


PEGUE AS FRASES NO PASSADO E ESCREVA DE FORMA NEGATIVA E DE FORMA NEGATIVA CONTRAÍDA.


I WAS NOT a teacher. / I wasn't a teacher.
You WERE NOT my friend. / You weren't my friend.
He WAS NOT in a happy day. / He wasn't in a happy day.
She WAS NOT very cool. / She wasn't very cool.
It WAS NOT a sunny day. / It wasn't a sunny day.
We WERE NOT all friends here. / We weren't all friends here.
They WERE NOT a tall and proud people. / They weren't a tall and proud people.




Frases na Forma Interrogativa (Pergunta)

WAS I a teacher?
WERE you my friend?
WAS he in a happy day?
WAS she very cool?
WAS it a sunny day?
WERE we all friends here?
WERE they a tall and proud people?
